04 · Working Principles

Responsible healthcare analytics

Performance numbers are only useful when the evidence behind them is clear.

01

Clinical Question First

Define the healthcare question, population, outcome and unit of analysis before choosing methods.

02

Data Quality

Document missingness, provenance, implausible values, coding assumptions and analytical limitations.

03

Reproducibility

Use documented workflows, version control, automated checks and transparent decision trails.

04

Clinical Governance

Predictive models require validation, calibration, subgroup assessment and governance before real-world clinical use.
